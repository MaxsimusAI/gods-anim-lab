# Checks, idea bank and proven phrases

Grows with every remark of Maxim (skill, section 3): what he saw becomes a CHECK question, a better way he named becomes an
IDEA. Recipes and questions — never bans on a means.

## Checks

One question per thing Maxim saw; "yes" always means the defect is there. The fresh-eyes reviewer asks 1-22 for every new
clip of every god (skill, gate G2) and 23-35 on the stand (gate G4), and all of them for the published clips of a god when
his turn comes. In brackets: where and when he saw it.

**Action clips**
1. Does only the acting arm move while the legs stand still? (Zeus TI atk, Kitsune atk/hyp, Athena BT atk, 13.11)
2. Does the god grow toward the camera, or look like a giant next to the enemies after the leap? (Athena atk, 12.11)
3. Is the move too abrupt, or does it jerk? (Athena atk 12.11; Athena TI ult 13.11; Hou Yi; Ra TI atk)
4. Does the body turn like a figurine on a stand, without stepping? (Van Helsing TI idle, four times)
5. Is a weapon held at full arm's length to the side or above the head? (Apollo's bow, Inti's staff, 12.11)
6. Is an arm fully stretched with the bow aimed flat at an edge? (Artemis 13.11, Hou Yi)
7. Is a hand, the head or about half of a weapon cut by an edge for half a second or more? (Zeus ult, 13.11)
8. Does the camera shake, or the figure jump in size? (13.11)
9. Is the weapon left unused — the bow not drawn, the effect born in emptiness? (Hou Yi, 13.11)
10. Does a melee clip read as a pose with a glowing weapon instead of a strike with the body behind it? (Athena BT atk, 13.11)
11. Is the super or the ult only a plain pose, without magic? (Van Helsing super, Amaterasu, Ra super)
12. Is the baked effect only "a little glow", hard to see at phone size? (12.11)
13. Does an effect burst in all directions up to the edges, or blind? (Apollo ult; Athena ult flash 12.11)
14. Is there a beam or stream that has nothing to do with what the skill does? (Athena super, 13.11)
15. Is the BT clip poorer in motion or effects than the TI clip? (13.11)
16. Is the recoil out of proportion to the weapon? (13.11)
17. Does the body vanish, turn transparent, or does haze or an explosion fill the frame? (13.11)
18. Does the sheet look softer than the live god at phone size? (13.11: «было размыто всё»)

**Idles**
19. Does the idle freeze, or "twitch and then stop for a second"? (Demeter TI, 12.11)
20. Do the legs stand still while only the head or the chest moves? (Zeus TI 12.11; Amaterasu; Agni BT)
21. Does it read as an attack or a dance? (Agni TI «слишком танцует»)
22. Is the weapon raised high?

**On the stand (game effects)**
23. Does the game effect start anywhere but the weapon or hand (`wf`) — chest, belt, neck, feet, emptiness?
    (Zeus belt, Erlang neck, Kitsune chest, Demeter feet, Athena not from the shield centre)
24. Does anything fly before the gesture is made, or does the damage land before the shot? (Brynhild TI super; Kitsune clone)
25. Is a baked effect that reaches an edge left without a game continuation from that point at that moment? (12.11)
26. Does the projectile clash with the weapon or the gesture — a slow ball from a drawn bow? (13.11)
27. Any cheap primitive: plain rays, pebbles, small plain balls, droplets, unclear symbols, jerky fire?
    (Athena ult rays and stones, Kitsune "5 little balls", Van Helsing symbol, Agni fire, Inti ball)
28. Is the melee leap slow, or does it miss the hit? (Brynhild atk)
29. A flash at the hits? (Hou Yi ult, Erlang super)
30. Is it unclear who does what to whom (leap / projectile / something flies to the allies)? (13.11)
31. Does the new god differ in height from the live one, or one style from the other? (12.11, 13.11)
32. Does anything on the stand cover the gods (health bars)? (12.11)
33. Is a translucent copy of a god drawn — doubles, after-images, ghosts? (Kitsune super and ult, 13.11 «выглядит ужасно…
    ни в каких героях не применять»)
34. Does the game draw a line, beam or bloom that the clip does not have, before its projectiles fly? (Kitsune super, 13.11
    «тонкая линия здесь не нужна»)
35. Does the game draw a symbol (mask, Medusa, moon, ears of wheat, name glyph) that the clip could carry itself? Then the
    symbol goes into the idea of the clip and the game stops drawing it once the clip has it. (13.11)

## Phrases from prompts whose clips were accepted

- `Wind-up, the first second: … Release: … Recovery, the rest of the clip: … settles into exactly the same pose as the first frame.`
- `She moves like a living woman, with natural weight shifts, bending knees and a supple spine; she never rotates rigidly like a figurine on a stand.`
- `Make every motion big, bold and exaggerated, with strong clear poses that read instantly even on a tiny phone screen.` (27.10, strikes)
- `In place, with his feet planted, he sinks into a deep crouch as a strong wind-up, gripping the sword with both hands in front of his chest` (Surt, 27.10 — "feet planted" worked here because the crouch is named)
- `she steps her shield-side foot forward and sinks down onto one knee … she kneels braced behind it like a wall` (Athena, 12.11)
- `During the skill her silhouette becomes narrower, never wider than at the start.` (Kitsune, 12.11)
- `The light stays on the shield: no beams, no rays flying away, no dome, no particles leaving it.` (Athena, 12.11 — a short ban
  about this clip's own effect worked)
- `Large, bright effects stay attached to his body, sword and feet; they may reach the edges of the frame and fade, but nothing is thrown or flies away.` (Surt, 27.10)
- Idle that moves enough: `shifts the whole weight from one leg to the other, a sway large enough to be seen from far away`.
- Against a white body in lightning: `lightning only on the fist and the staff, skin and robes keep their colours`.

Full texts: `pipeline/v2/tests/` (they are also the linter's "must pass" set).

## What did not work (so nobody tries it again)

- 27.10, version 4: "only slightly turns", "hand at chest height", "a small flash" → almost no motion. Version 5 ("big, bold",
  one physical action, in place) was accepted. The same over-constraint was repeated on 13.11.
- 13.11 R1: "feet planted on the same spot, faces the viewer the whole time" + 25 bans → glued feet, one arm moves.
- 13.11 R2: "the whole body moves: hops, spins, steps" + the same wall of bans → 16 of 16 action clips left the frame for 0.5-2 s.
- 13.11 R3: "no jumps, no floating, no spinning, steps only to the side, hand no higher than the head" → a static picture.
- "aim straight up" → Kling gives 50-60° at best (two tries).
- "arrow" in the text → the arm stretches and aims flat at an edge; "lightning" → bolts to every edge.
- The bundle prompt on the TI Kitsune → the tails opened wider than the frame, twice.
- Storing the strikes much smaller than the live game does (12.11: 130-174 px against 184-202) → «было размыто всё».
- NOT a failure — an open question. Storing a new strike at the idle's scale is Maxim's rule and is in force (skill B3).
  At 214 px in a 220 px frame about 3 % above the head is stored, so a move that needs the room above the head does not fit:
  the move changes. On 13.11 raised arms and hops were forbidden in the prompts instead — that gave the static clips (R3
  above). Storing strikes a little smaller is proposal 1 of the skill (section 10): not in force, not tested.
